# html-to-image

- Upstream: https://github.com/bubkoo/html-to-image
- Version: 1.11.13 (npm distribution `dist/html-to-image.js`)
- License: MIT, reproduced in `LICENSE` alongside the bundled script.
- Purpose: offline PNG export of rendered questions and answers, including
  embedded formula fonts and diagrams. No Node.js installation or CDN is needed
  by the user. The optional source-map comment is removed from the bundle.
